## Data Availability

The ECoG finger movement data used in this study are from BCI Competition IV Dataset 4 (https://www.bbci.de/competition/iv/#dataset4), provided by Miller and Schalk (Nature Human Behaviour, 2019). The dataset is publicly available subject to the original competition terms of use. Pre-processed spectrograms are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.
